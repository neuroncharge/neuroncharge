Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026. An overview of how we handle local calculations, analytics, and advertising data.

1. Local Processing of Cognitive & Calculation Data

The core functionality of our platform—including your mathematical inputs, physics calculations, and Neuron Metrics test results—operates entirely on your device. We do not transmit, harvest, or store the specific numbers you calculate or your cognitive test scores on our servers. Your progress is saved locally using your browser's native localStorage architecture, meaning you retain complete control over your historical data via the Insights dashboard.

2. Google Analytics (Usage Data)

To understand how visitors interact with our modules and to improve the platform, we use Google Analytics. This service collects standard internet log information and visitor behavior data in an anonymous form.

  • Data collected: This includes your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and the time spent on the site.
  • Opt-out: You can opt out of having your site activity available to Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. To serve these ads effectively, Google and its third-party vendors use cookies to track user interactions.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opt-out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device to collect standard internet log information. In addition to local storage for your metrics, our site uses cookies strictly for the analytics and advertising purposes mentioned above. You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies.

5. Your Data Rights

Depending on your region (such as under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Because your calculation and metric data is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete it immediately by clearing your browser data or using the reset function on the Insights page. For data collected via Google's analytics and advertising networks, please refer to their respective privacy tools and opt-out links provided above.

6. Policy Modifications

We reserve the right to update this policy as our infrastructure or legal requirements evolve. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page.
